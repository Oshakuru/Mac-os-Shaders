# macOS Shaders 1.9 — Pro preset, lighter wet ground, macOS Shaders naming

- Pro preset matches the author's in-game settings: shadow strength 1.0, shadow bias 1.4, 4 sunbeam steps at 0.30, one SDV cloud layer at 0.8 opacity, water reflections 2 with 4 trace steps, rain puddles and rain ripples off (ripple strength 0.3), Unobtrusive weather on.
- Wet ground reflections are off by default and in all three presets (Air, Pro, Pro Max), which skips the two wet-reflection passes. They can still be turned on under Atmosphere > Puddles and Wet Ground.
- Polish: the sky-state uniforms in the scene shader are declared in one place instead of three separately guarded copies; internal names left over from the Metal era (`metal…`, `M1_…`) are renamed to macOS Shaders; the Bloom tooltip no longer promises portal glow, which the portal texture is too dark to produce.
- The builder now runs the source and profile checks before packaging and writes fixed ZIP timestamps, so the same source gives the same ZIP. It also no longer forces Unobtrusive weather off: that option has used the game's own weather textures since 1.4, so the old weather-pack rule was undoing Pro's setting.
- Lava and the nether portal stay vanilla for now; a rework is on the to-do list. A molten lava option tried during 1.8 testing was removed.
- Links point to the renamed GitHub account, Oshakuru.

Recorded in the development history together with 1.8. Neither is published as a GitHub release; build from source to try them.

Source, profile and preprocessing checks pass. Not yet tested in game: the new Pro preset, and FPS against 1.7.

# macOS Shaders 1.8 — SDV-style nights, Miyazaki Skybox clouds, rain ripples (historical update, not released)

- Night only: the moonlight and night-fill scales were retuned so moonlit ground is roughly 5x brighter than shade (was about 2x) before tone mapping at the new defaults. Reference: Super Duper Vanilla's night contrast; no source copied.
- New night defaults in the shader and all three presets, chosen in game by the developer: Night ambient fill 0.80, Moonlight contrast 0.65.
- Art-style night colours, daylight, torches and menus are unchanged. Both night sliders keep working.
- With Enhanced lighting off (Air), moonlight contrast now reaches full strength at the default slider value.
- New Cloud style "Miyazaki Skybox": a pixel-art panorama of flat two-tone clouds in three depth layers at varied heights over a low haze bank, tinted by time of day, rain and art style like SDV Skybox and by the sky behind them. One texture read per sky pixel. The 2048x288 texture is original art generated by tools/gen_clouds.py in the style of pixel-art custom skies (reference: PP Custom Sky by XSSheep; no assets copied); it covers the full turn, has a fixed light direction, casts no shadows and cannot be flown through.
- Rain ripples fix: rings were only a bend in the water's reflection, which fades to nothing when looking down, so they vanished near the player. They are now also shaded directly on the water. The same rings now appear on ground puddles (with Rain puddles on), and bend puddle reflections in Sky +.

Night brightness was checked in game by the author with these values. The Miyazaki Skybox clouds in their final pixel-art form and the ripple fix have source and preprocessing checks only.

# macOS Shaders 1.7 update

- Increases direct moonlight by 20% before tone mapping while retaining ambient night darkness.
- Adds optional soft Miyazaki depth outlines, fading between 16 and 48 blocks. Toggle in Style Settings or Performance > Edge smoothing.
- Retains v1.6 water, foliage movement, and shadow algorithms, plus the v1.4/v1.5 multiversion compatibility patches.

Tested in game and accepted by the author. Source, profile, 576 outline-option and 6,960 compatibility preprocessing checks passed. No controlled FPS benchmark is claimed. Fine foliage edges can still shimmer with outlines enabled.

Night lighting remains a focus for the planned 1.8 update; no additional 1.8 night changes are included here.

# macOS Shaders 1.6

- Water tone now follows the art style: muddy and slightly murkier in Dark Fantasy,
  light blue and slightly clearer in Miyazaki. Vanilla keeps the 1.5 teal.
- Miyazaki carries 20% more contrast, built into the style; the Contrast slider still applies on top.
- Retains the multiversion compatibility patches from 1.4 and 1.5.
- No menu, preset or shadow-algorithm changes.
- Includes the author-supplied 1.6 settings; presets are unchanged.

The author has tried 1.6 in game and approved the new water tones, while reporting
foliage-shadow flicker. Comprehensive style/lighting checks and controlled FPS
benchmarks remain outstanding. The experimental static-foliage-shadow variant
was rejected after a reported performance drop and is not included in this baseline.

# macOS Shaders 1.5 — multiversion compatibility patch

- Extends the single-ZIP compatibility target to Minecraft 26.1, 26.2 and 26.3.
- Adds 26.3-only poplar leaf mappings (exclude puddles, retain foliage wind).
- Adds the red shrub to the existing small-plant wind/material mapping on 26.3.
- Retains mappings and weather fallback for Minecraft 1.20.1–1.21.11.
- No DH integration. Iris uses OpenGL; Vulkan is not a supported target.

Tested in game by the developer on Minecraft 1.20.1 and 26.3; versions in between
are covered by source checks only. See COMPATIBILITY.md.
