# macOS Shaders versioning

In development: **macOS Shaders 1.0**, not yet released. Last released version: **Metal 1.2**. After 1.0 ships, the next changed release is **1.1**.

The pack was called Metal through Metal 1.2. The author renamed it to macOS Shaders and restarted numbering at 1.0; macOS Shaders 1.0 follows Metal 1.2 and is unrelated to the earlier Metal 1.0.

Work continues under 1.0 until the author declares it released. After that, increment the minor version once per delivered revision: 1.1, 1.2, and so on, unless the author directs otherwise. Preserve the Metal 0.9.x, 1.0 and 1.2 development history and their `config/Metal-v*.zip.txt` settings files.
